Literal claude launch commands firstmate types into a crewmate pane
===================================================================
Produced by driving the real bin/fm-spawn.sh with a fake tmux that records the
`send-keys -l` payload - i.e. these are the exact command lines a crewmate,
scout, or profile-flagged ship spawn is launched with. Every claude kind now
carries --setting-sources project,local immediately after
--dangerously-skip-permissions, before --model/--effort.

=== crew (ship) claude launch, default profile ===
env -u CURSOR_AGENT -u CURSOR_INVOKED_AS CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_PROMPT_SUGGESTION=false claude --dangerously-skip-permissions --setting-sources project,local "$('<repo>/bin/fm-operational-input.sh' encode launch-brief < '/tmp/fm-crew-iso.ZzBt0s/ship/home/data/ship-z1/brief.md')"
